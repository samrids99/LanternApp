# ADR 001: Region-only location

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-03

## Context

Lantern is a social app for sharing places people have stayed in their vans. Publishing exact coordinates of quiet or wild spots can lead to overcrowding, landowner conflict and damage to the places the community values. Protecting those spots is a core principle of the product.

A hidden latitude/longitude column would still be a liability: it could leak through a bug, a breach or a misconfigured endpoint.

## Decision

Lantern will **not collect or store exact coordinates**. Each stay is linked to a user-selected **Region** (a hierarchical list, e.g. Scotland > Highlands > Applecross). The map shows regions using the region's own centroid, so every stay in a region appears at the same point.

## Consequences

- No PostGIS or geospatial queries needed for the MVP.
- The map shows region markers with stay counts, and tapping one opens a feed filtered to that region.
- Region data must be seeded from open data (e.g. OpenStreetMap, Natural Earth, ONS). Licences need checking.
- Region granularity needs care: too broad is useless, too narrow defeats the purpose.
- Photo EXIF data must be stripped (see ADR 004).
- Recommendations (pubs, walks, food) and free-text stories can still narrow a location. Mitigated with form prompts and a report/edit route, not eliminated.
- An optional "share exact spot with followers" feature is explicitly out of scope for the MVP.
