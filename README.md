# Lantern

A romanticised story-first vanlife app: share the places you loved staying, and the
stories that went with them. Region-only locations protect the quiet spots.

> Status: early development (MVP in progress)

## Why

Utility apps (e.g. Park4night) are excellent for logistics but cold. They answer “can I park here?” and not “what will it be like?”. Lantern allows the showcasing of sunsets and local pubs with a cosy hearth, offering a nice break from grey water dump sites.

## Tech stack

React Native (Expo) · ASP.NET Core · EF Core · PostgreSQL
